## Description (4000)

Stack, match, and don't let it fall.

Dropjong is a new kind of puzzle game where you stack Mahjong tiles on a table to build winning hands. The tiles roll, bump, and topple like the real thing. Keep your wobbly tower standing and go for a big hand.

HOW TO PLAY
- Pick one of the two tiles offered and drop it onto the table
- Drag to choose where it lands, then let go. You can turn tiles, stand them up, or lay them flat
- When the tiles on the table form a winning hand, you win the round: "Tsumo!" You score points and those tiles disappear
- Every tile that falls off the table costs a heart. The game ends when all 3 hearts are gone or the wall of tiles runs out

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
- Bigger hands score more. Mangan, Haneman, Baiman, and the legendary Yakuman come with special effects
- The sky and the music change as your score climbs
- Clear the whole wall to unlock Endless Mode
- Hand Collection: collect all 29 winning hands
- Game Center leaderboards (Normal and Endless) let you compete with players around the world
- 3 original songs. Score over 36,000 points to unlock the Music Box and listen to any of them

NEW TO MAHJONG? NO PROBLEM
- When you are one tile away from winning, the game shows which tiles you are waiting for
- It tells you when you make a hand, and explains what each hand means in a few words
- The Hand Collection shows how to make hands you haven't tried yet, with example tiles
- On your first games, a short guide walks you through choosing and dropping tiles
You will pick up the hands naturally while you play.

ABOUT ADS
The app is free to play. Ads appear on the title screen and the results screen. When you run out of hearts, you can watch a video ad to restore one heart, once per game, and keep playing.

NOTE
Your Hand Collection and on-device ranking are saved on your device. If you delete the app, they are erased (your Game Center leaderboard scores are kept).
